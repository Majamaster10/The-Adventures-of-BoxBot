# The-Adventures-of-BoxBot
An awesome and fun game, loved by all.

Game Description:
    
    
A hybrid action/adventure platformer following the adventures of BoxBot as it travels to the factory of the excessively polluting QBot, manufactured by the Qubit Corporation. Qubit Corp cares not about the harm it does to the planet and their recent edition of QBots are their most polluting yet. Due to an unforeseen reaction, the pollution outputted by the QBots have resulted in the malfunction in the main processing chip causing the QBots to attack anything that isn’t a product of Qubit Corp, including humans! BoxBot, a prototype created by Vertex Robotics, is a next generation robot that runs off of a nuclear proton core that produces 100 times more power than anything ever seen before and produces no harmful exhaust. BoxBot is now on a mission to fight, fly and push through whatever the armies of QBots throw at it to make it to the Qubit Corp factory and stop their harmful ways, with some help from a few upgrades the Vertex team develops.
